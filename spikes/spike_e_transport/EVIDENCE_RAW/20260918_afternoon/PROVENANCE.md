### Why the stub was rebuilt, and why the payloads are the same bytes

The 16/09 stub process (PID 28976) was still alive at 15:58, and `/health` still answered 200 from memory. But its payload directory `~/spike_e_payloads_20260916` and its worktree were no longer on disk, so a capture started at 15:58 returned **HTTP 404 on all 342 samples**. That capture is **not evidence** and is not included here. The preflight has since been changed to download one real slice per profile before measuring (`fdf7516`).

The payloads were regenerated at the same commit and compared field by field against the manifest the old stub still held in memory. Every volume, PNG, bitplane and mesh **size and triangle count is identical**, and so is the seed. The only difference is the mesh `source` label text: *"Spike E synthetic fallback — Spike B mesh unavailable"* now, *"Spike B SYNTHETIC mesh (…)"* then. Both runs served the same deterministic UV-sphere stand-in. Full volumes served from the rebuilt stub: **29,196,288 B** (`576x576x88`) and **36,044,800 B** (`640x640x88`).

## Limitations, stated rather than smoothed

- One afternoon window on one day. Together with 16/09 that makes two windows: evening and afternoon.
- One handset, one home uplink. The Mac mini's uplink state is not instrumented.
- Payload bytes are synthetic stand-ins with the Spike D `A6` shapes, as on 16/09.
- `cold_open_s1` has 3 samples per profile, the same as 16/09. Under the `E10` decision of 2026-09-18, `TC-PERF-FIRSTLOAD-01` needs its own loop of ≥ 20 cold opens per profile; this capture does not satisfy that and is not presented as doing so.
